Projects

PUNARVA: landslide survivor-detection rover

1st prize NSITE, 2nd place NIT Calicut

A rover designed to find people who are still alive and trapped inside landslides, combining ground-penetrating sensing with AI.

1st prize at the NSITE national hackathon (Nehru Group of Institutions). 2nd place in prototype demonstration at INNOVEX, Tathva, NIT Calicut. Selected for Stage 3 of the Vishwakarma Awards.

Edge-AI crop protection network

State winner, YIP (K-DISC)

A robotic system that detects animals at the edge of crop fields and scares them away using drones, rovers and hexapods, chosen to suit each crop. It also turns ordinary cameras into AI cameras that do security and animal detection.

State-level winner in the Young Innovators Programme. Presented as a paper at the Kerala Science Congress.

EXON: six-axis arm for non-planar 3D printingin progress

Python, PyBullet

Repurposing the open-source PAROL6 six-axis arm into a 3D printer with the nozzle in place of the gripper. Printing along curved paths in more than three axes cuts the support material that conventional printing needs, and the same arm can handle complex shapes for other applications.

EDNA: universal-input robotic arm

Self-designed, scaled-down six-axis arm

A scaled-down six-axis arm of my own design with a universal software adapter, so it accepts serial data from any control or slicing software.

Self-righting planetary roverin progress

Raspberry Pi, servo limbs

A rover that balances itself and gets back on its wheels after a roll or tip-over, using dedicated brace limbs. The goal is easier exploration of rough terrain that current rovers cannot cross.
